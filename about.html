OUR STORY

Made for the little pauses.

Some days are busy. Some are slow. Either way, a cup of coffee gives you a reason to pause for a moment. Coffelogy brings together familiar drinks, sweet treats and a simple way to discover your next favourite.

This website is a front-end project made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
